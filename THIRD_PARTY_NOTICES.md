### 1.4 "Snuff hosts" section — license not stated (maintainer action needed)

The final section of `scripts/hosts.txt` is introduced only by
`# Title: Snuff hosts` and carries no license, homepage, or author line.
It is currently included in the compiled list. Options for the maintainer:

- Keep it and document that domain-name lists are treated as factual data
  (no copyright claim asserted), or
- Remove the section from `scripts/hosts.txt` and regenerate
  `src/data/blocked-hosts.json` with `node scripts/parse-hosts.mjs`.

Contributors who are uncomfortable with this section should not be required
to redistribute it; removing it only affects which domains are always blocked.

## 3. Quranic content

- **Arabic text** (e.g. the Bismillah in the popup) is the text of the Quran
  itself and carries no copyright on the text.
- **English translation snippets** shown in the popup UI (a short rendering of
  the Bismillah and a phrase from Al-Baqarah 2:45) are brief quotations of
  published English translations. Copyright in such translations remains with
  their respective publishers; they are quoted here for attribution and
  interface purposes only.
- Verse text, full translations, and audio recitations used by the
  "listen to ayah" block mode are **not bundled in this repository** — they are
  served by the companion dashboard (see below). Their licensing is handled by
  that service, not by this extension package.
